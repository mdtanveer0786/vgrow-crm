# Project Overview: VGROW CRM

## 1. What does this application do?
VGROW is a modern, premium Customer Relationship Management (CRM) SaaS platform. It helps businesses manage their leads, track communication pipelines, monitor overall business health (Hygiene Score, Profile Health), and orchestrate their marketing and operational workflows from a single intuitive dashboard.

## 2. Who is the primary user and what is their core need?
The primary users are sales professionals, managers, and business owners. Their core need is to have a bird's-eye view of their pipeline, quickly understand which leads need attention, track their day-to-day tasks ("Today's Activity"), and maintain their online business reputation.

## 3. Core User Flows
- **Dashboard Overview:** User logs in, sees a comprehensive dashboard with Profile Health Score, CRM Hygiene Score, Today's Activity, and Getting Started modules.
- **Lead Management:** User can view, add, and update leads, tracking them across different pipeline stages (Warm, Hot, Prospecting, etc.).
- **Task Management:** User tracks daily tasks, meetings, and follow-ups.

## 4. Key Features (First Version)
1. **Interactive Dashboard:** Premium UI with glassmorphism, showing aggregate statistics, health scores, and quick actions.
2. **Modular Sidebar Navigation:** Clean, organized access to Pipeline, Communication, Commerce, Operations, HR, Recruitment, etc.
3. **Lead & Activity Tracking:** Full CRUD capabilities for leads, tracking next actions and temperature.

## 5. Explicitly Out of Scope (For Now)
- Real-time multiplayer collaboration on the same lead document.
- Complex third-party integrations (e.g., direct QuickBooks sync) until the core pipeline is stable.
